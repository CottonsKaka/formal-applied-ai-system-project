# PawPal+ Pet-Care Guidance

## Feeding
For routine feeding, use a short, realistic duration and include a clear meal description. Feeding can usually be scheduled near the owner's morning or evening routine. This guidance is general scheduling support, not medical advice.

## Walks and Outdoor Activity
Walk duration should match the activity, pet size, weather, and the owner's available time. A 20-to-30-minute walk is a common example, but an owner should follow veterinarian guidance for health conditions.

## Medication
Medication timing can be important. PawPal+ can create or move a medication task, but it must not interpret a prescription, determine a medical dose, or replace veterinarian instructions. If timing is medically prescribed, the owner should verify it directly.

## Grooming
Grooming tasks include brushing, nail trimming, bathing, or coat care. Short grooming tasks should not overlap walks or feeding when the owner is unable to supervise separate activities at once.

## Vet Visits
Vet visits should be treated as scheduled appointments with enough duration for travel and the visit when possible. Emergency symptoms require an appropriate veterinary or emergency service, not an application-generated routine plan.

## Conflict Handling
If tasks overlap, prefer a clear open slot found from the scheduler rather than silently changing a task. Preserve high-priority tasks when possible and report any task that was moved.
